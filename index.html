        ⚙️ Settings

        📺 Channel name
        Shows at the top of the app. If you're connected with your family code, everyone sees the new name!

        The settings below are just for you, on this device.

        👤 Your name
        Used for chat, comments, votes, and "Show cards for".
        🌙 Dark mode
        Darker colors, easier on your eyes at night.
        😀 Show emojis
        Turn this off if all the emojis are too much. Your ideas and messages don't change; emojis are just hidden.
        📱 Put it on your home screen
        iPad/iPhone: in Safari tap the Share button ⬆️, then Add to Home Screen. Android/Chrome: tap the ⋮ menu, then Add to Home screen. The app opens full-screen with its own icon! (It's a separate copy: connect your family code and set your name again inside it.)

        👨‍👩‍👧 Family Sync

        Type your secret family code so all three of you share ONE planner. Everyone must use the exact same code!

        🤫 Don't post your code anywhere public. Anyone who knows it can change your planner.

        📦 Share & Backup

        Save everything to a file to send to your siblings (or keep as a backup). They can load it into their copy.

        Importing adds the new ideas and cards to yours. Nothing you have gets erased; if the same card is in both, the imported one wins.
